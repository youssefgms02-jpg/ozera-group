Using OZERA GROUP.

These terms describe the basic expectations for using the OZERA GROUP website and sending a project enquiry.

Last updated: 4 November 2026. This is a general website-terms draft, not legal advice or a signed service contract. A parent or guardian and a qualified local professional should review it before accepting paid work or making it legally binding.

1. Website use

Use the website lawfully and provide accurate information when creating an account or submitting a request. Do not attempt to access another customer's account, interfere with the website, or send harmful or unlawful content.

2. Service requests and quotes

Submitting a request is an enquiry, not an automatic booking or acceptance of work. OZERA may ask follow-up questions before confirming scope, price, schedule, and deliverables. Prices shown on the website are starting estimates in Egyptian pounds and may change based on the final requirements.

3. Project scope and delivery

Before work begins, the customer and service provider should agree in writing on the scope, deliverables, revisions, schedule, responsibilities, and any third-party costs. Delivery dates are estimates until confirmed. Work outside the agreed scope may require a revised quote.

4. Payments

The website does not currently process online card payments. Do not send card details or passwords through website chat. Any payment method, amount, timing, and refund or cancellation terms must be agreed clearly before paid work starts.

5. Customer materials and rights

You should only submit text, images, logos, code, and other materials that you own or are permitted to use. The parties should agree in writing on ownership and permitted use of final deliverables before a project begins.

6. Third-party services

Some projects may depend on hosting providers, platforms, plugins, domains, or other external services. Their availability, pricing, and terms are controlled by those providers and may change independently.

7. Accounts and communication

You are responsible for keeping your login details private. Website chat is provided to discuss requests and project updates; it should not be used to send passwords, payment card details, or highly sensitive personal information.

8. Availability and changes

Website features may be updated, paused, or discontinued to maintain or improve the service. We will make reasonable efforts to keep submitted requests and account information available, but uninterrupted access cannot be guaranteed.

9. Age and guardian involvement

Users who are below the age required to independently enter into a service agreement in their location must involve a parent or guardian. Paid work, payments, and binding agreements should be handled with appropriate adult oversight.

10. Contact and updates

For questions about a request or these terms, use the contact method published on the website or your account chat. Updated terms will be posted on this page.
