Privacy & OZERA GROUP.

This page explains what information is handled when you use the OZERA GROUP website, create an account, submit a project request, or use website chat.

Last updated: 4 November 2026. This is a plain-language website policy draft. It should be reviewed by a parent or guardian and a qualified local professional before being treated as a formal legal policy.

1. Information you provide

When you create an account, the website may process your email address, optional display name, and optional phone number. When you submit a service request, it processes the selected service, estimated budget, project description, preferred timeline, and your chosen contact preference. If you ask to be contacted through Discord as well, the Discord username you provide may be stored.

2. Messages and support

Messages sent through the account chat are stored so you and the OZERA team can follow the conversation. Do not send passwords, payment card details, identity documents, or other sensitive information through chat.

3. Why information is used

  • Create and secure your account.
  • Receive, review, and respond to project requests.
  • Communicate about a request and show its status in your account.
  • Maintain the operation and security of the website.

4. Service providers

The website uses Supabase for account authentication and database storage, and Vercel for website hosting. Those providers process information as needed to provide their services under their own terms and privacy documentation.

5. Sharing and public visibility

OZERA does not intentionally publish account details, private project requests, or private chat messages. Information may be accessible to authorized administrators who need it to respond to requests and operate the service. The website does not currently offer checkout or collect card details.

6. Retention and account requests

Information may be retained while an account or project conversation is active and for a reasonable period afterward for continuity, security, or record-keeping. To request access, correction, or deletion, use the contact route shown on the website or send a message through your account. A request may need verification before action is taken.

7. Security

Access controls and database row-level security are used to limit access to account information. No online service can promise absolute security. Keep your password private and sign out on shared devices.

8. Younger users

If you are under the age required to independently agree to online services in your location, involve a parent or guardian before creating an account, submitting personal information, or entering into a project agreement.

9. Changes

This policy may be updated when the website's features or data practices change. The latest version will be published on this page.
